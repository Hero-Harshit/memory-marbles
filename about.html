Museum of Memory Marbles

Some memories glow yellow. Some burn red. Some settle into a quiet, aching blue. This is a place for all of them.

What is this?

Do you remember the first time something simple, a movie, a moment, a realization — quietly rearranged something inside you?

For me, it was understanding that emotions are not enemies to be controlled, but companions to be understood.

Museum of Memory Marbles was born from that feeling.

This is your space. A small, private corner of the internet where your memories exist without noise or judgment. No timelines. No algorithms. No performance.

Just you, standing in a quiet museum of your own life watching the past glow softly in your hands.

The Marbles

Every memory becomes something tangible, a marble.

🟡 Joy :- the warmth that lingers long after the moment passes

🔵 Sadness :- the depth that makes everything else meaningful

🔴 Anger :- the spark that tells you something matters

🟢 Disgust :- the instinct that protects you before logic can

🟣 Fear :- the quiet voice that still walks beside courage

Click a marble, and it opens. Not just as text, but as something you felt — something you lived.

Why I built this

As a frontend developer, I didn’t want to build something forgettable.

I wanted to create something personal. Something I would return to. Something that felt… alive.

Not productivity. Not optimization. Just reflection.

This project is imperfect, evolving, and deeply personal. And maybe — just maybe — it becomes something meaningful for you too.

Technical Details

Built with: Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Storage: Your data lives entirely in your browser (localStorage).

No servers. No tracking. No accounts.
